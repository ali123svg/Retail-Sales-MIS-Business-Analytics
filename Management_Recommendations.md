# Management Recommendations — Retail Sales MIS & Business Analytics

Based only on the actual findings in Business_Insights.md.

1. **Focus on East region recovery.** East trails the other 3 regions by ~40% in sales. Run a diagnostic (store count, staffing, local competition) before committing marketing spend, then pilot a targeted promotion in East before scaling regionally.

2. **Treat non-metro stores as a different market, not underperforming metro stores.** The Bottom 5 stores are all Tier-2/Tier-3 cities. Rather than pushing the same strategy as Bengaluru/Mumbai/Delhi, design a localized assortment and pricing approach for these stores.

3. **Protect and grow Apparel, Electronics, and Footwear.** These 3 categories drive the large majority of sales and quantity. Ensure they never go out of stock during peak months, and prioritize marketing spend here over lower-performing categories.

4. **Plan inventory and staffing around the Q4 surge.** Since sales jump sharply from October and peak in November, build up stock and temporary staffing in September, rather than reacting once the surge has already started.

5. **Prioritize Regular (individual) customer retention.** Regular customers generate ~74% of revenue, making them the core of the business. Invest loyalty programs and retention efforts here first, while still nurturing the smaller but valuable Corporate segment for growth.

6. **Increase order value from existing repeat customers.** Since the repeat customer rate is already high (~96%), the highest-leverage growth lever is cross-sell/bundle offers to existing repeat customers rather than pure new-customer acquisition.

7. **Clean up missing customer names in the data.** Fix the "Unknown Customer" data quality gap before presenting Top Customer numbers externally, so leadership sees accurate top-account rankings.

8. **Keep monitoring returns, but no urgent action needed.** At ~4.8% return rate and ~1.9% cancellation rate, both are within a healthy range — continue monthly tracking rather than launching a return-reduction initiative right now.
